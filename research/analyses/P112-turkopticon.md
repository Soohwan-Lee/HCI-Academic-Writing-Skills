## Contribution contract
The paper promises three things: a case study of intervening in a distributed microlabor system, an example of systems design that uses feminist analysis and reflexivity, and lessons from intervening at a large system's margins. Its central contrast is making relations visible and provoking debate rather than resolving political differences through design. An analytic thesis accompanies these: human computation currently relies on worker invisibility.

- **Methods (separate):** four years of participant-observation as design activists; informal AMT surveys, including 67 open-ended 'Bill of Rights' responses; employer fieldwork (four interviews, plus events and conversations); and maintenance and support encounters.
- **Form (separate):** a reflexive, first-person case narrative, not a controlled evaluation.

## Abstract and introduction moves
The six-sentence abstract moves through an attention gap, a re-framed object (AMT as worker-employer relations), the thesis, the artifact, a secondary affordance (mutual aid), and a forecast of sustaining challenges. The introduction opens with the 'frontier' framing and pivots to the labor organization enabling it. It then looks behind abstraction, presents hazards and response, gives adoption figures, claims a vantage point, and enumerates contributions. The roadmap comes unusually late, at the end of the stance section, so positionality precedes the system story.

## Evidence, warrant, limits
- **Invisibility claim:** Evidence is platform features, marketing language, and employer accounts. The warrant is infrastructure and agency theory. This is interpretive; it does not measure how far invisibility causes the field's practices.
- **Worker concerns:** Coded mentions (for example, 35 on unfair rejection) show what respondents raised under a deliberately provocative prompt, not prevalence. Coding is unreported and the five listed counts total 90, so categories presumably overlap.
- **Dispute and Amazon priorities:** Requester, CEO, and anonymous-source statements are participants' accounts. The inference about Amazon's incentives is the authors'.
- **System success:** Page views, installs, and press or speaking invitations show uptake and attention. They do not show changed requester behavior, better pay, or effective protection from retaliation; accountability and better behavior appear mainly as design aims or hope.
- **Solidarity:** 'Thriving', 'mutual aid', and strengthened ties are authors' interpretations from sustained involvement. No outcome measure is presented.
- **Illustration:** The India/US generosity contrast is hypothetical ('may feel'), although it follows a statement that deployment bore out concerns. It should not be read as a measured group difference.

## Findings-to-discussion transitions
The discussion recodes earlier narrative rather than adding results. Moderation, already described as a feature, returns as maintenance and then as solidarity work. The rating scheme becomes 'tactical quantification', a coined concept grounded in one design compromise, while 'generosity' becomes its stress case. Disparate Bill of Rights responses become an account of Turkopticon as an intermediate step toward publics. The final section then turns against the story of success: the tool also sustains and legitimizes the system it criticizes, and its funding is unsustainable.

## Transferable lessons and counterexamples
- **State the stance as a method consequence.** Explain how the authors' presence changes the phenomenon. *Counterexample:* reflexivity does not replace evidence for an effect claim.
- **Place theory where it does work.** *Counterexample:* a dispersed review fails if readers cannot locate the gap or the closest prior work.
- **Coin a concept from a constraint, and name its cost.** *Counterexample:* one compromise supports a proposed concept, not a general pattern.
- **Use a contrast paragraph to convert tension into an argument.** *Counterexample:* do not call something double-edged without stating both edges.
- **End by weighing success against complicity.** *Counterexample:* ambivalence cannot stand in for missing outcome evidence.
- **Report uptake precisely.** *Counterexample:* do not let adoption metrics imply impact.
## Integration review
The artifact, situated interpretation and political design lessons coexist. Do not route this as a conventional system-benefit experiment simply because software was deployed. Moderator involvement and maintenance evidence are more specific than a blanket claim of community ownership. The figure caption's assertion of protection from retribution is stronger than the body's stated attempt; the paper does not report a causal protection test. Keep its ambivalence and historical scope rather than treating 2013 platform details as current facts.
