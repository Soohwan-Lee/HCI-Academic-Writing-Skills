## Contribution and review subtype
A systematic literature review combines descriptive mapping with thematic synthesis of 42 empirical papers. It separates users' reported experiences from the perspectives researchers use to understand them. Its conceptual products include a three-stage reaction model and five perspectives: effect, agency, ethical, marginalization and creative labor. Discussion argues for moderated users as stakeholders and develops a research agenda.

This is not a pooled effect estimate. Qualitative, quantitative and mixed-method papers contribute different kinds of knowledge. The five perspectives classify scholarly framing; they are not five mutually exclusive user populations. The stage model synthesizes literature and does not establish that every person follows a measured causal sequence. The stakeholder proposal is a normative argument informed by findings, not a statistically demonstrated intervention benefit.

## Architecture and introductory progression
Public concerns → growth and synthesis need → separately motivated questions about corpus characteristics, experiences and conceptualizations → method and findings preview → contributions. Related work distinguishes moderation experience from operational challenges and nearby reviews. Methods decompose the broad experience question into who, what, where, how and subsequent reactions. Results follow those questions, then separate researcher perspectives. Discussion recontextualizes findings historically before proposing stakeholder relationships and future work.

The abstract names the scope, method, corpus and period, then moves from descriptive findings to perspectives and implications. It does not foreground every synthesis product. A writing assistant should help choose the central contribution rather than mechanically list every category or require every result in an abstract.

## Related work and the reason to review
The gap is a relationship among existing conversations, not an absence of moderation research. The nearest review is acknowledged and differentiated by analytical object: operational tensions versus user experience and how scholarship frames it. Within the background, communication, HCI and computational studies illustrate different emphases. These examples motivate cross-field synthesis; they do not establish exhaustive disciplinary stereotypes.

The review itself is research. Its related work positions the synthesis against adjacent syntheses; its findings analyze the selected corpus. Rewriting both sections as lists of the same papers would erase this difference in function.

## Selection and analysis warrants
The stated search window is January 2016 through March 2022; the earliest included paper is February 2016. Ten databases and two independent searches yield 7,924 records including duplicates. Initial filters leave 238; disagreement resolution and deduplication leave 52; seven citation-search additions produce 59; full-text exclusions leave 42. Root checked the rendered flow diagram and arithmetic. Distinguish retrieval records, retained papers, coded findings and participants in the underlying studies.

The included methods are 23 qualitative, 16 quantitative and three mixed. Full-text eligibility screening is assigned to the first author; two authors conduct thematic analysis. Full-paper inclusion is not a formal quality appraisal. PRISMA is a reporting framework, not a guarantee of synthesis quality or a substitute for the analysis account. The paper's comparison of PRISMA and Cochrane should not become a skill instruction.

The experience definition emphasizes first-person lived experience, while the corpus also includes behavioral traces and effect studies. An assistant should ask which evidence supports an experiential claim rather than silently treating every activity metric as a subjective account. Emotion analysis uses Plutchik-related keywords within an otherwise inductive account; describe the actual interpretive resources instead of claiming theory-free emergence.

## Discussion flow and a negative example
The discussion compares earlier community-management design with contemporary platform moderation, enumerates consequences, then develops communication, literacy and trust agendas. These are interpretive and normative steps, not just repeated frequencies. The p.19 first consequence paragraph has four sentences: claim, empirical example, connection to earlier design principles and an absolute counterfactual. The final step suggests that adopting those principles would have prevented the problems. That certainty is not warranted by the reviewed evidence. Preserve the useful claim–case–principle structure while making prevention a proposition to investigate.

The p.15 diagram uses directional arrows between emotions, perspectives and behaviors, with the last connection explicitly qualified. A diagram's sequence should not become a universal trajectory in prose. Distinguish an interpretive organization of studies from longitudinal transition evidence.

## Root-reviewed limitations and reuse cautions
- Corpus frequencies do not estimate prevalence across all platforms or users. A partial 2022 search period also limits annual growth comparisons.
- Categories can overlap. The increase/decrease activity lists reuse citations; adding category totals does not necessarily yield distinct studies. No complete citation-level reconstruction was performed here.
- Calling harms unintended requires evidence about institutional intentions. Harm reports alone do not establish intention or its absence.
- The search table visibly contains an unusual account-determination term. Preserve the reported query rather than silently repairing it and claiming replication.
- The first-person definition versus trace-based inclusion deserves explanation. Missing reports of appraisal or coding procedures mean not reported here, not necessarily not performed.
- Claude flagged further table, count and citation discrepancies. Only the flow, selected paragraph structures and issues described above were checked visually; other flags are not asserted as established corrections.

Useful transfer: separate the phenomenon from scholarship about it, justify the analytical difference from a nearby review, distinguish category counts from unique studies, and make the premises connecting synthesis to a normative agenda explicit.
