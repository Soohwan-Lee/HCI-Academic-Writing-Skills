## Architecture and moves

Actual sections are Introduction → Background → Methodology: Design Remake → The Undertable: A Design Remake and a Series of Counterfactual Artifacts → Reflexive Thematic Analysis and Theoretical Results → New Lenses for Homo Explorens → Remakes as a RtD Method → Conclusion; references precede an appendix summarizing the inherited framework. Within the artifact section, flimsy, nomadic, and subtle tables structure a chronological narrative; settings and participant accounts remain embedded in that trajectory.

The abstract introduces tables’ familiarity and social complexity, narrows to their underside, identifies the remake and tactile interaction, states the visual/textual account, then enumerates artifact, conceptual, and methodological contributions. The introduction begins with table idioms before connecting their secrecy and familiarity to the project. It promises a research journey and explicitly explains why conventional descriptions alternate with first-person stories. This is a counterexample to treating all introductory metaphor or narrative as dispensable ornament: here it introduces the material and conceptual premise.

Background moves from tables’ social organization to counterfactual artifacts, defamiliarization, under-table possibilities, and the specific original design. The comparison is central, not incidental citation padding. The paper explains how the new table changes the earlier wearable/performance setup while retaining bare-skin connection. Originality includes reworking and extending existing intermediate knowledge; the authors explicitly resist inventing another named concept just to appear new.

## Claim–support–warrant–limit

Design stories, prototypes, deployment observations, interviews, and reflexive interpretation support an additional lens, odd invitations, for homo explorens. They also make a case for remakes as engagement with design knowledge. The warrant is comparative: changing the form and setting reveals conditions of engagement that were less visible in the original. The formalized library study yields more interviews, while less controlled deployments yield memorable disruptions. The narrative preserves both benefits and failures instead of equating sample size with insight.

Section 5 identifies the designers as analysts and makes their subjective theoretical commitments explicit. The main analyzed data come from consented Kolgruvan and library deployments; earlier prototypes enrich the interpretation. The library convenience sample overrepresents interaction-design interest. Variations in climate, setting, facilitation, and relationships make direct causal comparison inappropriate. Section 6 earns its implications by returning point-by-point to the inherited framework before adding a fifth aspect. Section 7 separately argues for method-level value. These are distinct claims with related but nonidentical warrants.

## Transfer and exception

When contribution depends on emergence, retain consequential detours and failed expectations, explaining what each changes in the eventual claim. Use a comparison figure or explicit correspondence to keep remakes assessable. Do not convert these observations into guaranteed benefits of touching strangers or erase participants’ refusal. The openness of this account is appropriate to generative inquiry; it would not excuse missing controls in a causal comparison. A familiar object can warrant substantial background when its existing social meanings are the research material.
