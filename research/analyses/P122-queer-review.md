## Contribution contract
The review differentiates research substantially involving LGBTQ+ people from broader mentions, then examines how scholarly attention frames those lives. Its outputs combine a corpus and annotation approach, temporal mapping, interpretive research genres, critical provocations and recommendations. The method supports those outputs; using GTLR-inspired analysis does not automatically make the contribution a new grounded theory.

The search identified 1,148 publications across CHI, CSCW, DIS and TOCHI through 2022; 127 were excluded, leaving 1,021. Codes 4/3 contain 73/108 publications; codes 2/1 contain 450/390. Thus the two interpretive strands concern 181 and 840 publications. Extended abstracts, panels and workshops are deliberately included because community discourse matters. These are publications of mixed forms, not 1,021 empirical studies. Genre categories overlap; counts of identity categories also must not be summed as mutually exclusive populations.

## Section architecture
Introduction → background and language → identification/screening/organization/analysis/positionality → timeline → broader discussion of LGBTQ+ people → genres of queer-focused research → provocations for Queer HCI → recommendations for broader HCI → future work and limitations → conclusion. The paper answers RQ2 before RQ1 because peripheral discussion supplies historical context for focal research. Question numbering need not dictate the explanatory order.

## Abstract and introduction
The abstract establishes increased attention, a missing synthesis, venue scope, temporal and interpretive findings, then opportunities and a dataset. It foregrounds the distinction between research and discussion rather than detailing coding. The introduction juxtaposes attention and marginalization, distinguishes strands of Queer HCI, narrows to representations of people, states three RQs, explains their dependence, previews the corpus and findings, and names four contribution classes. Existing queer scholarship is the reason synthesis matters; a rewrite must not erase it with a blanket scarcity claim.

## Literature as background, evidence and analytical resource
There is no standalone related-work inventory. Language debates establish a writing policy; the corpus becomes findings evidence; situated knowledge, classification scholarship and prior genre work inform interpretation. The research-focus/application-area distinction is credited to prior sustainability scholarship. A review should distinguish analyzed sources from conceptual resources used to interpret them even when both appear in its references.

Positionality has procedural consequences: team experiences influence search terms and disagreements over whether a mention frames a group. Coding agreement on involvement does not independently validate the subsequent interpretive genres. PRISMA-informed selection and GTLR-inspired analysis serve different jobs; neither entails meta-analysis or universal checklist compliance.

## Micro-analysis 1 — p.12, §7.1, final paragraph
Five sentences: (1) concede strategic reasons to simplify group descriptions; (2) give a policy-advocacy example; (3) retain a boundary against erasing internal differences; (4) propose greater specificity through intersectional accounts; (5) connect that specificity to community self-understanding and a justice agenda. The concession carries a substantive political tension. Removing it for brevity would turn a conditional recommendation into a rigid prohibition. The prior paragraph establishes heterogeneity; this one prevents that argument from denying collective political action.

## Micro-analysis 2 — p.15, §9, paragraph beginning with method limitations
Six sentences: (1) introduce limits; (2) bound what published texts can reveal; (3) identify an inaccessible object, author motivation; (4) propose funding/publication pressure as a possible explanation; (5) state a normative commitment to treating people as ends; (6) derive future study of research institutions. The movement is evidence boundary → possibility → value → proposed inquiry. A fluent rewrite must keep these statuses distinct. The next paragraph introduces unpublished work and archival silence, extending rather than repeating the boundary.

## Findings-to-discussion progression
Four patterns in broader mentions contextualize five overlapping genres of substantive research. Genre headings identify framings, not natural kinds of people. Provocations then address specificity, trauma-centered attention and whose purposes the research serves. Recommendations change audience to the wider HCI community. This audience split gives each section an action recipient instead of repeating generic implications.

The critique of limitation sections is particularly useful for writing assistance: listing an omitted population is not itself an adequate explanation of scope, nor proof that the omission was acceptable. Conversely, the article explicitly recognizes thoughtful limits and research that uses a case population while still contributing knowledge about that population.

## Root-reviewed boundaries and counterexamples
- The introduction's 1,148-publication dataset statement must not replace the screened analysis denominator of 1,021. Figure 1 shows 38 unrelated-usage exclusions, while the text gives 37 as an example; preserve the version's discrepancy rather than inventing reconciliation.
- Raw publication growth does not establish growth in the proportion of all HCI output. Linear/exponential language is not a verified model fit here.
- A first occurrence or missing subgroup refers to searched venues, terms, period and involvement codes. It is not the beginning of queer scholarship or evidence that a community did not exist.
- The text itself says author motives and publication pressures cannot be inferred directly. Strong claims that trends drive researchers require that boundary.
- Normative political commitments are explicit parts of the argument, not statistical findings or defects to neutralize automatically.
- Table 4 distinguishes broad lesbian inclusion from exclusively lesbian-centered work, and includes overlapping identity categories. Do not flatten it into a ranking of community attention.

Transfer the layered analysis, contextualized critique and consequential concessions; do not transplant its political vocabulary or assume a fixed set of genres for another review.
