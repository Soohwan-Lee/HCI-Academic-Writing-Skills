# P84 — What is “Intelligent” in Intelligent User Interfaces?

Single-analyst rhetorical coding of the arXiv v1 author preprint (6 March 2020), not an assessment of a universal IUI template. Page numbers refer to this 20-page, single-column PDF. Both micro-passages were checked on rendered pages; paragraph boundaries are version dependent. The contribution is a corpus-grounded conceptual reflection, not a new intelligent system or a statistical synthesis of treatment effects despite “meta-analysis” in the title.

## Architecture and framing

The actual sequence is Abstract → Introduction → Background → Method → Results → Discussion → Conclusion → Acknowledgements → References. Background separates large-scale literature text analysis from definitions of intelligence; the latter divides human and technological intelligence. Discussion starts with limitations before advancing interpretations and future directions.

The abstract (p. 1) moves from an anniversary occasion and unresolved concept to a bottom-up strategy, extraction, three analytical questions, and the promised reflective use. It does not announce an accuracy improvement. Introduction (pp. 1–2) turns the apparent contradiction between a named field and an unsettled defining term into a research problem. Crucially, it anticipates the objection that terminological reflection is unnecessary: concepts shape assumptions, methods, metrics, and community boundaries. A second justification concerns interdisciplinary communication. A comparison with CHI’s coherence then narrows the question to IUI, before the method and findings appear. This is an occasion–puzzle–stakes–analogy–investigation opening, rather than an obligatory technical-deficit funnel.

Background (pp. 2–4) establishes two warrants. Prior text analyses justify observing a community through its published vocabulary, while human and technological definitions supply interpretive contrasts. Each cluster distinguishes corpus, unit of analysis, or perspective; citations establish what the proposed analysis can reveal rather than simply enumerating predecessors. The gap concerns tacit, distributed usage, not the total absence of intelligence definitions.

## Evidence, warrants, and limits

The authors assemble 1,111 full and short papers from 24 proceedings, extract sentences containing derivatives of “intelligen*,” and manually code entities, co-descriptors, and actions. Frequency, co-occurrence, examples, and temporal plots support claims about explicit published usage. The warrant connecting those observations to a community’s understanding is interpretive and bounded: language is evidence of communicated conceptions, not direct access to researchers’ private beliefs.

Limitations (pp. 11–12) explicitly address extraction errors, lost paragraph context, omitted synonyms, and inaccessible implicit meanings. The analysis of challenges is consequently not a comprehensive inventory of IUI problems. An accumulating vocabulary is also different from a statistically established temporal trend; the results report no clear patterns for several annual counts. This preprint inconsistently reports 1,804 versus 2,011 sentences and 59 versus 60 action categories. These discrepancies should trigger source verification before reusing counts; they should not be silently harmonized. No award is assumed here.

## Two complete paragraph micro-analyses

**Introduction, p. 1, second paragraph, opening “If we have managed”.** S1 poses the reader’s likely objection as a question. S2 answers by specifying consequences for research decisions. S3 connects that answer to prior reflection and gives the concept an instrumental role. S4 infers a reason for periodic examination. Relation: objection → consequential answer → conceptual warrant → justified action. The entry follows the definitional puzzle; the exit leads to the separate communication benefit. Its opening is interrogative, not a mandatory declarative topic sentence.

**Discussion §5.1, p. 12, final paragraph, opening “We favoured greater semantic”.** S1 states the methodological tradeoff favoring semantic depth. S2 restricts what the resulting evidence covers. S3 derives the missing-meaning limitation. S4 supplies an alternative operationalization through system characteristics. S5 explains why pursuing it exceeded scope. Relation: choice → boundary → consequence → alternative → scope justification. It follows narrower search-term exclusions and closes limitations before the positive interpretation in §5.2. This is productive qualification, not a detached apology.

## Transferable moves

Defend why a conceptual ambiguity matters before proposing a taxonomy. Align analytical questions with extraction categories and result subsections. Revisit early definitions in discussion so background becomes an interpretive resource. Place evidence boundaries before broad disciplinary implications. Preserve distinctions between observed vocabulary, inferred understanding, and recommendations: this paper offers a reflective reporting pattern, not a license to infer all community beliefs from keyword counts.
