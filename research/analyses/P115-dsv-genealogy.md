## 2. Contribution contract
**Promise:** a culturally situated explanation of why image-based DSV persists across technological change. The paper offers four eras, three constitutive dimensions (homosocial fabrication of obscenity, imperceptibility of violence, commercialization of abuse), a CSCW research agenda, and a case for genealogy.

**Method (separate):** a document-based integrative review of 47 academic papers, 59 investigative articles, and legal materials, followed by timeline building, thematic analysis, four analytic lenses, and weekly author discussions (October 2024–May 2025).

**Form (separate):** chronological periodization, then a cross-era analytic essay.

**Not promised:** participant data, a built system, or prevalence or causal estimation.

## 4. Abstract and introduction moves
The abstract moves from persistence to four eras, sources, three dimensions, future directions, and method reflection, then adds a content warning.

The introduction opens with a protest quotation and scene, then moves from the 2024 deepfake scandals backward to Nth rooms and spycam protests. That sequence creates the puzzle: why does harm persist despite reform? Paired questions introduce genealogy. The paper then previews eras, dimensions, and contributions, and pre-empts a linear reading.

Positionality is treated as an interpretive resource, not a validity guarantee. “Observe” means reading documents; survivor and police statements arrive through journalism. Panopticon, rhizome, and foot-in-the-door are interpretive lenses, not measured mechanisms. Table 1 is explicitly non-exhaustive, and the periodization is explicitly analytic rather than complete.

## 7. Findings-to-discussion transitions
§4 narrates by era; §5 deliberately “leaps” across eras to convert cases into dimensions. Section 6.1 then repeats a three-part pattern: restate the dimension, name the research assumption it unsettles, and propose a direction. Obscenity shifts inquiry from image content to relational construction. Imperceptibility challenges consent-based design. Rhizomatic commerce shows punishment is insufficient and points to restorative work.

Modality weakens appropriately from “demonstrate” to “can benefit” and “could consider.” These implications remain unvalidated. Section 6.2 turns from phenomenon to method, while “root causes” remains a proposal, not a tested capability.

## 10. Transferable lessons and counterexamples
- **Separate the chronicle from the argument.** Use one section for cases, another for concepts. *Counterexample:* short papers may need integrated case-analysis sections.
- **State what periodization does not mean.** *Counterexample:* “progressively” and “increasingly” can reintroduce linearity, as in §5.2.
- **Attribute evidence by institutional voice.** “According to prosecutors” preserves epistemic status. *Counterexample:* “Anyone could generate” generalizes beyond a reported case.
- **Make implications structurally parallel.** *Counterexample:* parallelism can make uneven evidence look equally strong.
- **Use a foil to create a gap.** *Counterexample:* do not promise a framework extension that the paper never develops.
- **Treat positionality as analytic work.** Explain what it enabled. *Counterexample:* it cannot replace an audit trail from lenses to dimensions.
- **Let moral stakes speak with precision.** Evaluative prose fits this topic. *Counterexample:* phrases like “undoubtedly” can outrun documentary evidence.
## Integration review and classification boundary
Primary claim: culturally situated sociotechnical genealogy, with historical characterization and critical reframing. Documentary synthesis is its method; it is not automatically a conventional literature-review contribution. Its reflection on genealogy demonstrates a use and argues for value, without proving superiority to other methods. Root does not adopt Claude's blanket theory-generative label: the paper develops interpretive dimensions with existing lenses, and a stronger theory-generation label requires an explicit argument about what theory is produced.

The paper contrasts integrative and systematic review using source breadth, but a systematic review can include grey literature; do not teach academic-only selection as a defining rule. Its non-linear commitments also sit beside progressive language, and era labels vary between text and table. Preserve reported versus independently established numbers. Critical consent arguments are the authors' normative/conceptual reasoning, not measured victim attitudes or legal advice. Reward recognition does not make these tensions disappear.
