## Architecture and framing

The paper proceeds through Introduction → Background (Internet of Things; Philosophies of technology; Thing perspectives in HCI) → The Morse Things → Our Study (Deployment; Workshop) → Data Analysis → Findings → Discussion → Call for Future Research → Conclusion. The findings are grouped as searching for humanness, thing-centeredness, and tensions between these positions. The discussion reinterprets these through withdrawal, attachment despite incomprehension, and a possible new category of domestic thing.

The abstract starts with approach and artifact, explains the networked ceramic objects, identifies six expert households and six weeks, introduces the subsequent workshop, and ends with the conceptual yield. The introduction likewise begins with the project rather than a broad problem. It subsequently expands to IoT history, recognizes the value of human-centered design, and asks what that orientation may leave unseen. The contribution statement on p. 504 promises an empirically derived description and a possible category, not proof of nonhuman consciousness.

Background differentiates a thing-centered inquiry from cameras that provide another view of human routines and from technically connected products serving predetermined human functions. Philosophical sources are operational resources for interpreting relationships, not decorative authorities. The gap is a limit of perspective. The paper explicitly preserves the value of human-centered approaches rather than dismissing predecessors as inadequate in every respect.

## Claim, support, warrant, limits

Material speculation produces counterfactual objects whose computational lives do not serve their owners. Deployment sensitizes expert participants; a six-hour workshop elicits situated accounts and generative concepts. Collaborative analysis uses grounded-theory coding techniques. These are methods and evidence sources, whereas the contribution concerns relations and attachments revealed through the inquiry. The paper explicitly treats much deployment documentation as priming material and reserves fuller analysis for future work (pp. 507–508). That boundary is essential when representing the evidence base.

The warrant is that a deliberately strange yet livable artifact can expose assumptions through sustained encounters and informed co-speculation. Quotes, proposed designs, contradictory reactions, and attachment incidents support the interpretive account. They do not demonstrate representative public acceptance or validate a market product. Discussion p. 511 moves from philosophical withdrawal through participant anthropomorphism to qualifications and concrete attachment, while p. 512 explicitly disavows commercial viability as its objective. The expert sample enables conceptual engagement but also bounds transfer.

## Paragraph micro-analysis

**P. 503, Introduction, “The emergence of IoT” (nine sentences; visually inspected).** S1 introduces critical reflection; S2 gives a cited control-oriented example; S3 introduces a manifesto; S4 explains its human principles; S5 synthesizes their shared value; S6 states the human-centered aim while explicitly declining to challenge it; S7 asks what that perspective hides; S8 locates the project in that question; S9 restates the question positively as what another perspective could disclose. Relations: topic → examples → synthesis → concession → question → positioning → constructive reformulation. This is not conclusion-first: the key research opening arrives after fair reconstruction of existing commitments. The previous paragraph supplies the IoT trajectory; the following paragraph sharpens the paradox of living with things without fully knowing their perspective.

## Transfer and exceptions

Use related work to distinguish what a lens makes visible, not only what a system can do. Explain why participants are epistemically appropriate to the intended contribution, and distinguish priming, data collection, and analysis. A findings-to-discussion transformation can be visible in changed headings: empirical tensions become conceptual relationships. Do not turn the three findings themes into universal RtD headings, infer that all deployment materials were equally analyzed, or translate speculative ontology into measured mental states. For optimization claims, the same deployment/workshop structure would provide insufficient comparative evidence.
