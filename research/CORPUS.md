## Coverage and exclusions
CHI 5, CSCW 4, DIS 2, IUI 2, UIST 9.
Many contribution routes remain incomplete during expansion. Do not infer typical
venue styles or statistical norms. “Unverified” does not mean non-awarded.

Candidate P10 (arXiv:2310.19778) was obtained while looking for a synthesis
example but not analyzed or included: its eventual venue is outside the requested
five. P11 replaced it because its group-recommendation topic and CSCW publication
fit the request. Candidate downloads are private and excluded from Git.

Search used public scholarly indexes, author/institution pages, arXiv, official
conference award pages and ACM metadata. Zotero item retrieval timed out; its
contents were not used to claim personalized sampling. P07 is an institutional
teaching copy rather than author-hosted; DOI/title and conference identity match.
A preprint and version of record may differ. Counts and coding apply only to the
listed checksum/version. No PDF or extracted full text is redistributed here.

## Reading the evidence
Each analysis distinguishes the authors' claims, our interpretation, the support
used, and its limits. Paragraph examples are selected cases, not frequency data.
Award records and lab affiliations are discovery/provenance metadata, not an
instruction to imitate particular authors or assert their endorsement.
