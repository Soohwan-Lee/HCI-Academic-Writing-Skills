# Second-expansion candidate queue

This is a **screening queue, not the analyzed corpus**. The first 30 candidates
were found independently through official program/award records on 2026-10-30.
Titles and recognition categories were matched there; publication identity,
full-text access and contribution fit still require review. Selection questions
are hypotheses for reading, not completed classifications. No citation count is
claimed. These candidates are distinct by normalized title from the 108-paper baseline.

The queue currently covers four venues and emphasizes recent decorated work.
IUI, 2018–2023 influential papers, non-awarded contrasts, resources, replication,
measurement and other Zotero collections still require dedicated screening.
This initial queue is not yet balanced against the [allocation](../docs/EXPANSION-200.md).
It must not become the entire sample merely because awards are easy to locate.

See [machine-readable queue](expansion-candidates.json) for per-paper source,
selection question, category and status. Keep private library membership outside
this file; public bibliographic information can be added after verification.
