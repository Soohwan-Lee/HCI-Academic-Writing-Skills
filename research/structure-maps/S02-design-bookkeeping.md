# S02 — Reflective pictorial with repeated cases and cross-case interpretation

Source: [P128 Design Bookkeeping](../analyses/P128-design-bookkeeping.md), DIS 2024
pictorial, NSF-hosted PDF. Re-read structure and mapped passages 2026-10-31.
All twelve argument pages were previously visually inspected; source page numbers
below are PDF pages, not printed proceedings pages. Labels are paraphrased unless
needed to identify a source heading. The artifact is context for reflective knowledge,
not a system awaiting a generic usability section.

## 1. Actual hierarchy and reading path

| Location | Structure | Reader's changing question |
|---|---|---|
| p.1 | Abstract and Introduction | Why does material-led design need this account of supporting work? |
| p.2 | Finished installation and visual/contextual presentation | What work and material complexity are we trying to understand? |
| p.3 | Situated collaboration with exploration diagram | Who made it, under what conditions, and how did the inquiry unfold? |
| p.4 | Why bookkeeping? → artifact-as-ledger lens → six-case introduction | How did the reflective lens emerge; how will the cases be read? |
| pp.5–10 | Six case pages, each with claim-like title, identified entries, origin account, interpretation, numbered visuals/captions | What happened here and what does this instance make intelligible? |
| p.11 | Discussion opening → cross-cutting themes | What relates these heterogeneous cases and what kind of knowledge is this? |
| p.11, within themes | Genre/ledger account → transfer trajectories → short dimensions roadmap → scope, audience and relation to ideation | How is this knowledge organized, for whom, and with what relationship to designing? |
| p.12 | Creating/sharing ledgers → Conclusion | How might this practice circulate or be cultivated? |

There is no separate conventional Methods–Results–Evaluation sequence. Method
and conceptual commitments are articulated before cases. Literature occurs where
it positions documentation forms and informs the lens. Case pages combine making
and interpretation; discussion changes analytical scale from particular entries
to a genre and practice. Run-in labels on p.11 are not numbered subsections.

A purely chronological reconstruction would obscure the cross-case relation.
A conventional system paper's feature/effect structure would also misidentify the
contribution: the final installation is not itself the sole knowledge object.
An alternative could introduce the lens before the installation, but would need
concrete material context early enough for the otherwise mundane records to matter.

## 2. Subsection and paragraph roles on the conceptual page, p.4

| Unit | Complete local paragraph sequence | Dependency |
|---|---|---|
| Why bookkeeping? | ¶1 retrospective recurring moments → metaphor; ¶2 first-person retrospection/questions → methodological position → neighboring documentation forms and closest portfolio precedent | Explains why this lens and account are appropriate, without inventing a predetermined protocol |
| Artifact-as-ledger | ¶1 define the installation as ledger and documents as entries, then establish their practical necessity; ¶2 introduce outside conceptual resources, develop the clinical-record analogy and refine the inquiry | Definition makes the later analogy and case descriptions interpretable |
| Six-case introduction | Opening paragraph names the collections; four-item list specifies title, material, emergence and interpretation; closing paragraph explains the cases' shared purpose | Creates a reader contract that each visual case then fulfills |

The long method/positioning paragraph has multiple linked jobs. A drafting agent
may propose splitting it to clarify boundaries, but must mark that as an editorial
alternative rather than claim the source uses separate methods and related work.

## 3. Complete local paragraph map: first case page, p.5

The claim-like title states that entries emerge through sense-making. A short
entry descriptor identifies bespoke weaving notation. These are structural units,
not additional body paragraphs.

- **Origin paragraph:** explains the mismatch between embodied weaving and standard
  notation, describes degrees of freedom, and introduces a representation that
  serves both imagining and machine instruction. It answers why these records arose.
- **Interpretation paragraph:** names the reciprocal relation between sense-making
  and documentation, directs attention to numbered materials, situates their
  nonstandard form in a community of practice, and draws a representational opening.
  It answers what the records teach the reader.
- **Visual/caption sequence:** carries a path sketch through fabric layers and a
  machine draft to process and finished cloth. It makes translation inspectable.
  Captions are evidence connections, not an optional illustration after the prose.

The two paragraphs have different verbs and temporal orientations: emergence and
use are reconstructed, then their significance is interpreted. Repeating the
production story in the second paragraph would remove the case's knowledge claim.

## 4. Complete local paragraph map: cross-cutting themes, p.11

1. Locate bookkeeping as a documentation genre, distinguish entries from ledgers,
   and explain non-linear adaptation through a metaphor with an acknowledged limit.
2. Identify multiple knowledge-transfer trajectories, audiences and expertise
   requirements; explain the value of a collection while retaining co-presence's role.
3. A short orientation names three dimensions for the following paragraphs.
4. Scope: explain provisional solutions and how adaptation can extend them.
5. Audience: explain self-addressed records, shared embodied expertise, depth and
   the different value available to readers outside that expertise.
6. Relation to ideation: explain mutually shaping practices and their significance
   for design research.

This is **genre → circulation → comparison axes → dimension-by-dimension
interpretation**, not six more case summaries. The next subsection changes from
interpretation to cultivation/sharing proposals. Its paragraphs progress through
before/during work, after work, research publication and circulation outside academia.
Those are prospective orientations, not four evaluated intervention conditions.

## 5. Sentence flow in the case, p.5

**Origin paragraph, five sentences:** (1) embodied-practice mismatch; (2) contrast
between a simple formal grid and richer decisions; (3) concrete yarn/path choices;
(4) the representation developed to explore these possibilities; (5) its dual role
in imagination and machine translation. The subject chain moves from notation to
weaving actions and back to representations. The concrete third sentence supplies
the need that the fourth answers.

**Interpretation paragraph, five sentences:** (1) reciprocal conceptual claim;
(2) explain the emerging notation through image references; (3) identify the
process/sample photographs; (4) qualify the notation's standardization while
locating a shared practice; (5) open a way to rethink representation. The initial
claim is intelligible because the preceding paragraph and visuals supply its case.
Moving it to the beginning of the entire page without context would change its job.

## 6. Contrast and conditional transfer

Unlike S01's overview → rationale → implementation → capability evaluation, this
case uses artifact/context → reflective lens → repeated emergence/interpretation
cases → cross-case concepts → possible circulation. Both have artifacts, design
processes and figures; their epistemic roles differ.

This pattern fits situated reflective practice with an inspectable set of materials.
It is not a universal RtD form: longitudinal deployments, somaesthetic inquiry,
speculative encounters and participatory change may need different temporal and
relational organizations. Seek those contrasts before generalizing from this case.
