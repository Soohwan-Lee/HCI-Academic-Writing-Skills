# Award verification — official programs first

Updated 2026-10-30 following the user's clarification. Use `programs.sigchi.org`
conference award pages and paper/session records, followed by the conference's own
annual website and official archive. Match the title **and authors**, venue/year,
and the award heading or badge attached to that paper. A search hit containing a
nearby award label is insufficient. Prefer the original year's record if a later
archive has ambiguous formatting. Keep paper, student-paper, demo, talk and
retrospective impact awards distinct.

## Negative checks and unresolved scope

Screen2Words, inFORM, Sauron and ERICA were not found in the inspected annual
Best Paper/Honorable Mention sections. Their records retain `unverified` plus a
specific check result; this does not claim they never received any recognition.
The UIST 2013 archive's flattened year/category sequence was ambiguous for Soylent,
so the original UIST 2010 page resolved its exact student-paper category.

This pass verified seven previously unresolved original-year awards, taking records
with established original-year paper recognition from 28 to 35. Seventy-two retain
`unverified`; this is **not a completed audit of all 107 records**. The two later
impact recognitions are separate. Previous verified records were not all re-audited.
Other queried annual pages produced irrelevant results, missing pages or program
entries without sufficient award evidence, so no award was inferred from them.

Future passes should work by conference/year, record access failures, and distinguish
not checked, inaccessible, absent from a specified list, conflicting evidence and
positively verified recognition. Neither award status nor its absence changes the
need to assess a paper's actual writing and knowledge contribution.
