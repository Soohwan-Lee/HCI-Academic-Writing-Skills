# HCI Academic Writing Skills

Evidence-linked writing skills for researchers working in ACM LaTeX projects,
with planned support for Codex, Claude Code, and Antigravity.

[한국어](README.ko.md) · [Development plan](docs/PLAN.md) ·
[Paper corpus](research/CORPUS.md) · [Review log](docs/REVIEW-LOG.md)

**In development.** 104 full-text analyses have been reviewed and integrated.
The release target is **at least 100 papers** across CHI, CSCW, UIST, DIS, and IUI.
Pending analyses and reserved candidates are excluded from the completed count.
Installation and writing assistance are still being validated.

## What this project is building

- Common guidance for argument structure, abstracts, introductions, related work,
  paragraph progression, sentence connections, and evidence-bounded claims.
- Distinct writing routes for quantitative and qualitative findings, systems,
  design knowledge, conceptual arguments, methods, resources, and literature reviews.
- Practical workflows for outlining, drafting, revising, compressing, and responding
  to reviewers while editing an existing multi-file ACM manuscript.
- Transparent sources and original analytical notes, with explicit exceptions and
  limitations. Awards and reputation guide discovery; they do not establish rules.

The corpus spans HCI beyond any single research topic. Word-count measurements
will describe their actual samples; adaptable 8,000-word budgets will be labeled
editorial planning aids, not universal publication norms.

## Follow the work

[PLAN](docs/PLAN.md) tracks milestones and current decisions.
[SAMPLING-PLAN](docs/SAMPLING-PLAN.md) defines coverage and inclusion requirements.
[REVIEW-LOG](docs/REVIEW-LOG.md) records checks before integration.
[CORPUS](research/CORPUS.md) links reviewed paper analyses and provenance.
Small reviewed batches are committed and pushed separately.

Only original analyses and public bibliographic metadata belong in this repository.
Source PDFs, full-text caches, private Zotero data, and model transcripts stay local.
The final bilingual documentation, installation guide, and visual identity will be
completed after research synthesis and workflow validation.
