# Audience and coverage decisions

Updated 2026-10-30. The user requested both personal relevance and a public toolkit
for other HCI researchers. These goals are complementary: personal research
provides realistic tasks; it does not define the limits of the paper corpus.

## Personal use cases, from public sources

[Soohwan Lee's research overview](https://soohwan-lee.github.io/) emphasizes
AI-mediated group decisions, social influence, multi-agent systems, minority
participation, power asymmetry, and reflective interfaces. These motivate examples
that distinguish individual response from group outcomes, compliance from durable
attitude change, and a system's intended support from demonstrated social effects.

The [publication list](https://soohwan-lee.github.io/publications/) also includes
reflective journaling, group dance practice, nonverbal communication and sensing.
The [project descriptions](https://soohwan-lee.github.io/portfolio/) connect these
to conceptual work on collective experience, visualization and embodied interaction.
These are topic/genre pointers, not full-text analyses or evidence of study results.
Under-review titles are not treated as accepted publications. Companion/workshop
papers are not silently counted as full research papers.

## Consequences for sampling and skill design

- Include a meaningful cluster of group collaboration, social influence, human–AI
  interaction, reflection and embodied coordination exemplars. Retain related
  quantitative, qualitative, conceptual and artifact routes rather than one
  compulsory architecture for this cluster.
- Include public, clearly hypothetical task examples in those domains, alongside
  accessibility, health, privacy, fabrication, learning, community and design cases.
  Never use private manuscripts or unpublished results as public fixtures.
- Track venue, primary knowledge contribution, secondary outputs, method, paper
  form, domain, time period and discovery rationale separately. A validation study
  does not automatically add an empirical knowledge contribution to a system paper.
- Audit concentration by lab and topic before synthesis freeze. Add missing
  contrasts rather than relabeling papers to fill target cells.
- Venue targets are planning guides, not evidence that venues determine style.
  CHI subcommittees can aid topical discovery but are not the contribution taxonomy.
- Make the core skill useful without knowing this author's identity. Personal
  use cases belong among examples; required inputs are the user's actual writing
  task, claims, evidence, manuscript and target venue.
