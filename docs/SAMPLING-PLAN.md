# Expanded sampling and analysis plan

Updated 2026-10-30 after explicit user direction. Supersedes the 10–15 paper
pilot target in PLAN/PROTOCOL. **Minimum release target: 100 full-text analyzed
papers.** The initial ten are a pilot, not sufficient coverage for release.

## Selection priorities
1. Knowledge contributions and evidence practices, not the user's present topic.
2. Well-written, substantial exemplars discovered through verified Best Paper /
   Honorable Mention lists, established research groups, foundational work and
   methodologically informative contrasts. Reputation is a discovery route, not
   evidence of writing effectiveness or endorsement.
3. Broad HCI domains: accessibility/disability, social computing/work, health,
   education, creativity, fabrication/tangible interaction, input/interaction
   techniques, sustainability/more-than-human design, privacy/security,
   visualization, human–AI interaction, games and community participation.
4. Zotero is optional candidate discovery, not a boundary around the corpus.
5. Record author-stated contribution, analyst label, method, and support separately.
   Permit hybrids, but do not count a validation study as another knowledge claim.

## Minimum analysis per included paper
- Public bibliographic identity, analyzed version, full-text URL/checksum.
- Actual section order, abstract and introduction moves, related-work function.
- Claim→support→warrant→limit; how discussion earns its implications.
- At least one paragraph micro-analysis with all sentence roles and adjacent links.
- At least one transferable decision and one context/exception where not to use it.
- Source locators, method vs contribution distinction, award evidence or unverified.
- Full-text reading required: abstract-only or metadata-only items remain candidates.

Deep sample: at least three papers per route with two or more paragraph examples,
including a non-deductive example where present. For lengths, measure only reviewed
native-source or visually verified boundaries; report n and selection separately.
Length coverage may be smaller than rhetorical coverage and must be stated.

## Batch process
1. Assign nonoverlapping paper IDs and topical responsibilities.
2. Obtain and read; record failures/exclusions, never silently substitute metadata.
3. Write original analytical notes and metadata fragments.
4. Parent review: identity, locators, claims, duplicates, route/domain coverage.
5. Integrate approximately 4–6-paper batches, update coverage table, commit and push.
6. Before skills freeze: compare across at least three examples per route; identify
   robust shared decisions, exceptions and unsupported hypotheses.
7. Claude critiques synthesis and practical LaTeX workflows; blind behavioral
   checks test concrete manuscript tasks, not just agreement with our own rubric.

## Award-source refinement
Prefer official SIGCHI program award pages and annual conference records when
verifying recognition. Keep original-year paper/student-paper awards separate from
later impact, demo and talk awards. Record the access method and unresolved cases;
see [award verification](../research/AWARD-VERIFICATION.md).
