# Qualitative and interpretive empirical writing

Use this module when the paper contributes an account of experience, practice, meaning, organization, or social relations through interpreted empirical material. Counts, document analysis and workshops can also belong here. Route by what readers learn, not by whether a method is called an interview, ethnography, or co-design. Consult [hybrids](hybrids.md) when a conceptual framework, design resource, or artifact has an independently substantive contribution.

Examples are selected observations, not universal conventions or award-proven strategies.

## Align question, access, interpretation and contribution

Before editing existing ACM `.tex`, identify the inquiry, empirical setting, researcher relationship, sampling/recruitment, materials, analytic approach, findings, and contribution. Preserve participants' wording, pseudonyms, attribution, citation keys and methodological commitments. Do not silently rewrite a reflexive interpretation as objective discovery or invent missing quotations, observations, negative cases, saturation, or validation procedures.

Write a working alignment row for each intended claim: **question → what the materials provide access to → analytic warrant → claimed understanding → boundary**. Interviews can support accounts of experience and participants' interpretations; they do not automatically establish behavior observed directly. Documents reveal recorded priorities and representations; unreported practices are not proven absent. [P16, Do Datasets Have Politics?](https://doi.org/10.1145/3476058) interprets disciplinary values in documentation. Its object is documentary practice, not direct observation of every dataset creator's decisions.

Name the conceptual role honestly. A theory may motivate the question, sensitize analysis, organize interpretation, be revised, or be an output; these are different relationships. [P36, Ocean Science Coordination](https://doi.org/10.1145/3432955) applies articulation work rather than claiming to refine it. Descriptive inquiry may need little theory. Do not impose a grand theory, inter-rater reliability statistic, codebook format, or saturation claim to make different qualitative traditions look alike. Describe the rigor actually appropriate to the stated approach and actually performed.

## Select an adaptable architecture

A practical default is introduction/positioning → context and method → findings → discussion → conclusion, with boundaries and positionality placed where they affect interpretation. Context may need its own section before readers can understand the activity. [P38, Weaving by Touch](https://doi.org/10.1145/3313831.3376477) explains weaving mechanics before analyzing tactile interaction. That explanation earns space through its later evidentiary use.

For linked inquiries, show what phase one changed in phase two. [P40, DIY Empowerment](https://doi.org/10.1145/3064663.3064674) includes a distinct phase-linking section between research process and findings. For complementary analyses of shared materials, identify each analytic question and then integrate their outputs; do not imply independent samples. For an interpretive case, a narrative sequence may carry the empirical argument better than uniform theme subsections. [P34, Wikipedia Order](https://doi.org/10.1145/1718918.1718941) organizes around the work of a case and develops trace ethnography as a separate methodological claim.

Findings and discussion may be integrated when the epistemology and argument require it. If separated, give discussion a new job: theoretical comparison, cross-theme interpretation, boundary, critique, or research/design orientation. Do not duplicate every findings heading with “implications” appended. P16 moves from observations at three levels to value tensions, changing the level of analysis.

## Draft abstract, introduction and related work

An abstract can identify the phenomenon and consequential tension, name the setting and approach, state the central interpretation or findings, and explain what that understanding changes. Describe the contribution with content: “reveals challenges” is less informative than naming the relationship or tension. Avoid promising a validated intervention when the paper offers situated understanding and prospective directions.

Build the introduction around a specific interpretive need: an accepted assumption fails to capture an experience; a known practice is misunderstood; existing accounts omit important actors; or a useful concept needs examination in a consequential setting. Explain why this site, participant group, or material gives access to that problem. [P06, Questioning the AI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3313831.3376590) justifies practitioners as intermediaries between needs and technical capabilities, then explains the probe used to support inquiry. Its analyzed [arXiv v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.02478v3) includes a later resource revision; do not treat it as identical to the conference copy.

Explicit RQs are useful when they clarify scope, but are not compulsory. A vignette, positional opening, or conceptual tension can also work if it leads to a clear inquiry. Distinguish present contribution from a longer program's ambition: [P35, Coping with Interpersonal Racism](https://doi.org/10.1145/3392828) separates understanding support practices from future intervention development.

Related work should connect conversations through the question they help answer. Identify what each strand supplies: context, conceptual vocabulary, an unresolved disagreement, methodological precedent, or comparison case. State what the paper extends without treating predecessors as uniformly deficient. P38 connects disability-making, materiality, and ability-diverse collaboration; these intersections return in its findings and discussion. The gap may be a limited explanation or standpoint rather than an untouched population. Keep novelty claims bounded to the literature actually examined.

## Write findings that earn interpretation

For each finding, specify the analytic proposition, show situated evidence, explain why that evidence supports it, and retain variation or contradiction. Tell readers whose voice they are hearing: participant account, fieldnote, documentary observation, or researcher interpretation. Introduce enough context for a quotation to be intelligible; interpret it rather than ending with it as self-evident proof. Several shorter examples may show variation, while a sustained episode may reveal coordination that fragments would lose.

Choose organization according to the knowledge: patterns across cases, a process over time, contrasting positions, or a detailed case followed by analytic synthesis. Counts can locate the material, but do not turn purposive samples into population prevalence. An unusual case can expose a boundary without needing to be the most frequent. Distinguish participant aspiration from the authors' critique, as [P33, Indie Game Labor](https://doi.org/10.1145/3392864) does when examining inclusion and economic exclusion.

Discussion should explain what becomes newly understandable, how the interpretation relates to prior accounts, and where transfer might fail. Engineering recommendations are optional. The contribution may instead challenge a value, expose institutional work, or offer an ethical tension. P38's analysis of labor valuation remains valuable without a feature list. If proposing a design, link it to the relevant finding and label its effectiveness untested. Avoid turning situated adaptations into universal instructions.

## Paragraph and sentence progression

Use these role sequences flexibly; they are not fixed sentence counts or mandatory deductive templates.

- **Context to inquiry:** available practice → unresolved use → consequential work → actors with access → study focus. P06, introduction p. 1, builds its methodological consequence at the end rather than stating a result first.
- **Interpretation with a counterexample:** preceding synthesis → exceptional case → concrete evidence → interpretation → remaining qualification. P16, discussion §5.1 p. 25, prevents a broad reading of neglected care from becoming exceptionless.
- **Benefit to political tension:** practice → concrete benefit → structural complication → interpretive consequence. P38, discussion p. 10, examines how recognition and selling can both enable participation and reproduce problematic valuations.
- **Critical comparison:** participant aspiration → evidence of constraints → interpretive tension → bounded question. P33, §5.3 p. 19, changes from reporting participants' projects to examining their limits.

A topical opening identifies the subject; a conclusion-first opening states the paragraph's substantive takeaway. Choose deliberately. Check the preceding paragraph's unresolved issue and the following paragraph's purpose to ensure the connection is conceptual, not merely “furthermore.”

## Original hypothetical revision

**Hypothetical illustration, not a source finding:** suppose supplied interviews describe reassurance from shared checking alongside concern about surveillance.

Before: “Participants liked collaboration. This demonstrates that shared dashboards empower patients.”

After: “Participants described shared checking as reassuring when they could choose who saw the information. Accounts of unwanted monitoring complicated that reassurance. We interpret control over sharing as part of what made support acceptable in these interviews; the accounts do not establish that a shared dashboard would improve care.”

Attach actual excerpts and relevant differences only from supplied materials. This revision names the interpretive relationship and its boundary without fabricating participants or measured outcomes.

## Workflows and reviewer checks

**Outline:** align questions, access and warrants; choose thematic, process or case organization. **Draft:** build evidence-bearing findings, then interpret their relation to the literature; revise the opening to promise that understanding. **Revise:** inspect attribution, theoretical role, counterexamples, positionality and claims that outrun the setting. **Compress:** remove repeated theme summaries and background that does no later work before cutting contextual details necessary to interpret an episode.

Check: Is the sampling logic intelligible? Is analysis described beyond a method label? Are quotations doing evidentiary work? Are silence and absence overinterpreted? Is the researcher perspective visible where consequential? Does discussion add an interpretation rather than repeat themes? Are design implications being forced? Would the contribution remain clear if an unevaluated resource were removed? Report missing evidence as an editorial issue while completing supported revisions.
