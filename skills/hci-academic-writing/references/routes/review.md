# Literature reviews and research synthesis

Use when the literature itself is systematically or purposively examined to produce
new knowledge. A paper containing many citations is not necessarily a review.
Document analysis can instead investigate publication practices as social evidence.
Clarify the review question, intended synthesis and coverage claim before selecting
a reporting structure.

## Match the form to the question

| Intended knowledge | Likely synthesis form | Main writing obligation |
|---|---|---|
| What research exists, where and how? | Scoping/systematic mapping | Explain scope, selection, categories and coverage |
| What do eligible studies establish about a focused question? | Systematic review | Align eligibility, appraisal, extraction and synthesis |
| What effects can comparable studies jointly estimate? | Quantitative meta-analysis | Explain comparability, effect metrics, dependence, uncertainty and bias |
| How do concepts, assumptions or traditions differ? | Critical/conceptual synthesis | Explain selection, interpretive commitments and argumentative warrant |
| How has a field represented a practice or community? | Structured review with interpretive analysis | Separate reported discourse from underlying practice |

When retrieval/selection procedure is unknown, call the work a literature review
or descriptive synthesis provisionally. Absence of pooling does not establish that
a review is systematic, scoping or mapping; confirm the actual procedure before
using a narrower label.

These are overlapping orientations, not labels assigned by a diagram or database
search alone. Do not call descriptive aggregation a pooled-effect meta-analysis
because a source uses that word. [P62, Fitts' Law in XR](https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3713623)
illustrates that distinction. For actual meta-analysis, use the research team's
statistical expertise and current relevant methodological sources; this writing
module does not prescribe a pooling model from genre alone.

## Write a review contribution beyond collection

State what the synthesis makes newly understandable: a taxonomy, tension, theoretical
relation, pattern of evidence, methodological limitation or justified agenda. Counts
and a search flow are supporting work, not automatically the intellectual gain.
[P61, Accessibility Research](https://doi.org/10.1145/3411764.3445412) connects field
patterns to critical questions about participation and research foci. [P11, Group
Recommendation](https://doi.org/10.1145/3555161) organizes interaction design knowledge.
A low count only establishes rarity within the selected corpus; explain separately
why the gap matters.

## Architecture and opening

A practical sequence is motivation/question → conceptual background and prior
reviews → selection/analysis method → descriptive map and synthesis → interpretation,
agenda and limits → conclusion. An interpretive review may put crucial limitations
before findings because they determine what a frequency means. A conceptual review
may weave scholarship through its argument rather than use a conventional Results
section. State the chosen form and why it fits.

An abstract should identify the question, scope, corpus/units, synthesis operation,
central insight and consequence. Keep paper, study, participant and dataset counts
distinct. In the introduction, explain what previous syntheses cover and what the
new question adds; “there is no review of exactly these keywords” is insufficient.
Related work supplies concepts, neighboring reviews and analytical commitments;
make it distinguishable from the included corpus used as evidence.

## Method as a warrant for the synthesis

Report the actual sources, search dates, queries/fields, eligibility, screening,
deduplication, access failures and selection decisions needed to assess coverage.
Explain why restrictions serve the question and what they omit. Search strings and
flow totals should agree across abstract, methods, tables, appendices and conclusion.
An inaccessible source stays unavailable, not silently analyzed from its abstract.

Do not infer screening or coding procedures from counts alone: knowing there are
143 publications and 152 studies does not establish that every study was coded
independently. Mark unavailable procedural details instead.

Describe extraction units, variables, interpretive lenses, category development,
reviewer roles, disagreements and changes. Distinguish screening consistency from
the validity of interpretations. [P67, Community-Collaborative Approaches](https://doi.org/10.1145/3491102.3517716)
separates systematic retrieval from reflexive thematic analysis. Do not require the
same agreement statistic for every analytical tradition or invent checks not done.
[P52, Democratic Participation](https://doi.org/10.1145/3359241) reports a single
classifier and overlapping categories; retain those facts rather than polishing
its method into an imagined consensus protocol.

Appraisal should match the review's claim. Peer-reviewed inclusion and reporting-
guideline compliance do not themselves establish unbiased studies or a complete
search. For quantitative synthesis, report the actual treatment of study quality,
heterogeneity and uncertainty. If absent, identify the inferential consequence;
do not manufacture a retrospective assessment.

## Findings, interpretation and agenda

Organize findings around the questions, not the spreadsheet's column order. Define
categories, show examples and account for overlap. Pair counts with the meaning of
the classification. Explain denominator changes and missing data. [P61] uses
different temporal windows for detailed coding and broader historical counts;
those evidence resolutions are not interchangeable. [P65, Creativity Support Tools](https://doi.org/10.1145/3290605.3300619)
explains why a pooled participant average would be misleading.

Discussion connects patterns to a framework or substantive consequence. For an evidence-derived agenda, a useful chain is **observed pattern → why it
matters → research opportunity → what evidence would address it**. A critical or
normative agenda may instead state its theoretical/ethical premise and show how
the proposed direction follows; make that warrant explicit. Do not infer an intervention's effect from
cross-study differences in confounded designs. Distinguish absence in publications
from absence in projects, and absence in the selected corpus from absence in HCI.
Citation-based selection, as in [P51, Futures Review](https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3713759),
limits prevalence and temporal claims even when it provides useful exemplars.

## Paragraph flow and original example

An extraction paragraph can use **analytical purpose → schema → precision limit
→ procedure → synthesis consequence**. P62's terminology paragraph pairs merge
and exclusion rules with examples, making construct comparability visible. A
limitation paragraph can use **restriction → missing material → possible distortion
→ implication for the claim**, as P52 does for venue selection.

**Hypothetical revision:** “Only three papers study minority participation, proving
that HCI neglects minority voices” becomes “Three included papers explicitly
analyzed minority participation under our coding definition. The search boundary
and reporting practices may omit other relevant work. This sparse coverage motivates
closer examination of how participation is operationalized, rather than a field-wide
claim that the issue is ignored.” Replace the illustrative count with verified data.

## Execute and review

Outline the question–selection–analysis–claim chain. Draft methods from actual
records and findings from checked extraction. Build interpretive synthesis before
writing the agenda. Reconcile every corpus count, date and unit; verify citations
and source roles. Compress repetitive study descriptions into synthesis, retaining
examples needed to substantiate categories. Keep full search/coding material in
appropriate appendices or supplements when permitted. In `.tex`, check flowchart,
table and appendix references after changes. Never fabricate a screening record to
make an incomplete review appear finished.
