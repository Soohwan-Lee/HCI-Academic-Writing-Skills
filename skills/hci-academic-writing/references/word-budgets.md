# Word budgets and compression

Use an explicit budget as a planning tool, then adapt it to the argument and the
venue's current instructions. Neither the corpus size nor an award establishes a
universal section ratio or ideal paragraph length. Most analyzed papers are coded
for rhetoric; only a smaller, declared sample has reproducible length measurements.
Do not represent all analyzed papers as a quantitative style survey.

## Define what is counted

For an existing draft, measure its current section allocation first and propose
changes relative to it, with a reason for each shift. Use the table only to initialize
a blank outline or when the author requests an illustrative allocation.

Before measuring, state whether the target includes abstract, captions, tables,
footnotes, acknowledgments and references. A PDF extraction count, TeXcount count
and submission-system count can differ. Avoid promising an exact venue-compliant
word count from a raw extraction. In an existing project, inspect its own counting
configuration and use the same convention for before/after comparisons.

The plans below allocate **8,000 main-body prose words**, excluding abstract,
references, captions, tables, acknowledgments and appendices. They are original
editorial starting points, not observed averages or ACM rules. Every row sums to
8,000; merged/renamed sections are legitimate. If the user's 8,000 includes an
abstract or captions, reserve those first and scale the remaining budget.

| Route | Introduction | Literature / framing | Main development | Evidence / findings | Discussion / limits | Conclusion |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Quantitative | 850 | 1,050 | 1,800 method | 2,000 results | 2,000 | 300 |
| Qualitative | 800 | 1,200 | 1,300 method/context | 2,900 findings | 1,500 | 300 |
| Artifact | 800 | 950 | 2,600 system/design | 2,100 evaluation | 1,250 | 300 |
| Design / RtD | 750 | 1,100 | 2,700 process/artifacts | 1,700 annotated encounters | 1,450 | 300 |
| Conceptual | 800 | 1,400 | 3,200 argument | 1,000 worked cases | 1,300 | 300 |
| Method | 750 | 1,100 | 2,300 procedure/rationale | 2,300 validation | 1,250 | 300 |
| Resource | 750 | 900 | 2,800 construction/documentation | 2,000 characterization/use | 1,250 | 300 |
| Review | 750 | 850 | 1,650 protocol/analysis | 2,800 synthesis | 1,650 | 300 |

Here “evidence” does not prescribe empirical testing for a conceptual argument,
nor a separate findings section for a design narrative. Split the main development
by argumentative job; do not impose these labels on the manuscript. Two dependent
studies can share method detail. A systematic review with complex selection may
need more protocol space; a rich ethnography may need more contextualized findings.
For any reallocation, say what reader need earns the extra space.

An abstract may be drafted provisionally at roughly 150–250 words when no limit is
yet supplied, but verify the actual track and author preferences before treating
that as a target. This range is a drafting convenience, not a corpus finding.

## Paragraph length without an invented optimum

Use length to flag a possible problem, not to diagnose quality on its own. A short
paragraph can state a transition, research question or consequential qualification.
A longer one may develop a complete episode or comparison. Ask whether it has one
coherent job, enough support and readable internal structure. Selected measurements
include short and extended paragraphs; their purposive selection cannot establish
an ideal range or a mandatory number of sentences.

For an outline, budget by jobs: for example, four introduction moves within 850
words might require five to seven paragraphs of unequal length. Do not multiply a
fixed paragraph word count by a fixed number of paragraphs and call the result a
venue norm. If providing a provisional paragraph target, label it an editorial
choice for that specific passage and explain its purpose.

## Compress while protecting the contribution

First remove repeated motivation, duplicated findings, generic significance claims,
software-output narration and examples that perform the same job. Merge near-duplicate
literature paragraphs by their common claim. Move implementation detail to an
appropriate appendix only if the venue allows it and the main argument remains
inspectable; do not hide essential methodological choices or adverse outcomes.

Keep operational definitions, assignment/sampling logic, uncertainty, denominators,
important counterexamples, provenance and evidence needed to assess the claim.
Avoid compressing several citations into a sentence whose claim none actually
supports. Preserve context needed to understand a participant excerpt or design
encounter. For every large deletion, check whether an opening promise now lacks
support. Return achieved length under the stated convention and any remaining
overage; do not claim compliance when only approximate counting was possible.
