## Consequences

The catalogue improves discovery in these examples but does not establish reliable
claim preservation. Do not report an overall success rate from eight purposive,
non-independent prompts. Known unsupported additions remain in T1, consistent with
prior [semantic trials](SEMANTIC-REPORT.md). T6 cautions against turning methodological
care into a universal test checklist. These failures remain regression cases for
the expansion; a later rerun must be recorded separately rather than replacing them.
