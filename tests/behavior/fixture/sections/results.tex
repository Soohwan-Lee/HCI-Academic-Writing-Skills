\section{Results}\label{sec:results}
% Synthetic fixture values supplied for testing; not findings about real participants.
The study included 24 participants who completed both conditions. Participants achieved a mean task score of 7.4 in the assisted condition and 7.1 in the baseline condition. The difference in task score was not statistically significant ($p=.42$). The assisted condition also required more time: participants spent a median of 18 minutes in that condition, compared with a median of 12 minutes in the baseline condition. In addition to this time cost, participants reported higher effort in the assisted condition, with median ratings of 5 and 3 on the seven-point scale, respectively. These results do not establish a task-score benefit for the assisted condition, and the additional time and effort should be considered when interpreting its use.
